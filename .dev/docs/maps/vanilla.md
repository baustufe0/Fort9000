## Starting fort

Plan coordinates of the game's `ai/forts/vanilla/mid` plans, team 1, enemy to the right; weapon_pack's ToGAF1/ToGWM2
use the same fort, shifted by -3950/-3300.

- Rock under the fort from x -3600 to -2900 (top y=700). The prow (x -2945..-2845) already overhangs the cliff:
  anything heavy out there tears the fort apart. The fort leans forward, so build backwards and upwards.
- Reactor at about (-3040, 305); roof beam (-3093, 205)-(-2945, 206) with posts at both ends; front posts
  (-2945, 206), (-2943, 312), (-2892, 411) … down to (-2900, 700).
- Starting devices: 2 mines (x -3157, -3284), a battery, a store, a machine gun on the lower front strut
  (-2996,504)-(-2895,505) (`orig_devices`; not on the prow).
- Free mine sites: x -3029, -3412, -3539 on y=700 (a player also mines x -2902, under the front; unused so far).
- Proven slots from the game's plans: battery/store, workshop, upgrade, factory (Template-1/-2), munitions plant and a
  third workshop slot (ToGWM2).
- Economy: start 601 metal / 3004 energy, +13 metal/s (reactor 5, mines 4 each), ~+100 energy/s; caps 900 / 5000.
- `rebrace_after_mine` (map default, nodes 50-53, 53-55): the inner armour layer in front of the reactor is rebuilt as
  wood after the first mine for metal; the outer shell at x -2845 stays. Vanilla-2 and -5 turn it off: in two lost
  Vanilla-2 matches the breach came through exactly that layer. Vanilla-3 does it at once (plan step `rebrace`).

## Tower (Vanilla-3)

Shared with the cannon plan Vanilla-4 (removed 28.10.2026; tests in
[history/vanilla-3-5.md](../history/vanilla-3-5.md)).

- **Shape:** a back block on the ground (x -3600..-3100, back to the rock edge) up to the starting fort's roof (y=205),
  and a solid, fully X-braced weapon tower (x -3400..-2945) on the back block and the starting fort's roof posts. No
  prow storeys and no woodspam base on the prow (the prow's armour reached 0.97 of its breaking limit under a cannon).
- **Rows** 205, 105, -20, -145, -270, -370 (tower roof), cage -500: the heavy-weapon storeys and the sniper storey
  below them are 125 high (a heavy weapon's box reaches the floor 100 above once nodes sag, and the game turns the
  overlapped floor into background bracing); 155x125 cells have 199-long diagonals.
- **Storey by storey** (`tower_connect`): a cell in storey k waits for every tower cell in storeys 1..max(2, k-2), so the
  back part (on the back block) and the front part (on the starting fort) are joined before the tower rises; built
  column by column they leaned apart and squeezed the seam column x -3300..-3200. Cost: ~155-170 struts before the
  first roof turbine.
- **Turbine cage** on the tower roof: a 130-high storey of background bracing (`hidden_box` x -3400..-2945, y
  -500..-370), two turbines on its floor and two stacked sandbags in its split front cell (x -3100..-2945) right after
  the first turbine; left out of `tower_connect` (`skip_floors`). Two turbines only (the seam column refuses one,
  `turbine_avoid`), upgraded late from an upgrade centre with storage and anti-air in between. The first turbine
  comes after 152-162 struts; the sandbag cell costs ~23 struts.
- **Heavy weapons** stand at x -3200..-3100 (behind the 155-wide front cells, which are their fire lanes:
  `mark_large`, door in front, background diagonals), LinkT 0.5, one above the other. Stacked in the weapon column with
  100-high storeys, or one column back (the seam), the upper one was refused.
- **Snipers:** never in the front cell on the starting fort's front post (x -3100..-2945, floor 105; refused in every
  match, `weapon_avoid`). Vanilla-3 (and the former laser plan) put the sniper in the front column at floor -145 (`weapon_avoid` keeps the
  machine gun out; built), at LinkT 0.28 (`sniper_t`) so its split door's brace clears it. In the top front cell (floor -270;
  the removed Vanilla-4) it was refused once the tower stood below it.
- **Doors:** `split_doors` cuts the door posts to the shot: Vanilla-3's 20mm doors 78 instead of 125 (126 shots
  through them), its sniper's 62.5. Cannons (the removed Vanilla-4) and the former laser kept full doors (no split covers
  both barrel estimates); in the 100-high storeys no brace clears the sniper, so its door stays full.
- **Woodspam** (region 0): two layers (x -2905/-2865) in front of the tower front, rows -370..-145, late. Regions 1 and
  2 (Vanilla-3, since 29.10.2026): Vanilla-2's lower-face layers and the extra mine-row layers, right after the second
  mine (it had nothing there and fell to one laser sweep along the mine row).
- **Late block:** every plan ends with more anti-air, weapons, upgrades and storage, so the AI does not sit idle at the
  storage cap.

## Vanilla-5 – rocket rush

Replaced the laser rush on 26.10.2026: a laser took about five minutes on Vanilla and the laser plan lost all four
matches against an active player (its tests and lessons: [history/vanilla-3-5.md](../history/vanilla-3-5.md); the
plan file kept its name, so `train.py`'s wins per plan mix both plans).

- Idea: EMP rockets as early as possible, a smoke launcher beside them and anti-air behind them; the EMP rockets fire
  while the upgrade centre is built and are then upgraded to rockets. The AI fires the smoke first and holds the
  rockets until they are under smoke, so the enemy's anti-air cannot see them ([ai.md](../ai.md#fire-control),

  was refused (first test).

## Vanilla-6 – modular fort

- The idea: a fixed plan loses to whatever the player picks against it (Vanilla-5 lost to four different weapons).
  Vanilla-6 keeps a fixed core and turns its weapons into reactive build modules ([ai.md](../ai.md#reactive-build-modules)).
- Geometry: Vanilla-2's low fort, strut for strut (the most tested layout; the hidden mortar nest and turbine gallery
  two storeys over the starting fort, nothing in the mortars' arc); the weapons stand where Vanilla-2's stand.
- Core (231 actions): workshop; the sniper's cell (built for a sniper: door, background diagonals) and the nest cell
  with the split mortar struts (`build` step, before anything else builds them); the nest's sandbags; three mines;
  a turbine; the gallery's sandbags; woodspam; two turbines; upgrade centre; battery, store; upgraded turbines; a
  battery; upgraded mines; woodspam.
- Modules (enemy devices, or at the latest the time; `Requires` = plan action):

  | Module | Builds | Enemy builds | Time | Requires |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | sniper | sniper on the starting fort's roof beam | sniper, AP sniper, laser, firebeam, cannon, 20mm | 45 s | 5 |
  | artillery | two mortars in the nest | mortar, heavy mortar | 150 s | 30 |
  | antiair | machine gun on the gallery roof | missile, EMP missile, rocket, EMP rocket | 240 s | 145 |
  | heavyartillery | heavy mortars (after artillery) | - | 330 s | 199 |
  | antiair2 | second gallery-roof machine gun (after antiair) | 3 of the missile types | 420 s | 192 |
  | sniper2 | AP sniper (after sniper) | 2 AP snipers, lasers or firebeams | 450 s | 199 |
  | miniguns | the front gallery-roof machine gun and the starting fort's upgraded (after antiair2) | - | 540 s | 199 |
  | artillery2 | two more mortars on the gallery roof, between the machine guns (after heavyartillery) | - | 480 s | 189 |
  | heavyartillery2 | their upgrade (after artillery2) | - | 560 s | 199 |
  | frontlayer | a third woodspam layer (x -2772) in front of the starting fort's lower front (y 411..700) | 6 machine guns or miniguns | 600 s | 167 |

  Against a player who builds nothing the AI still reaches its goal, Vanilla-2's artillery fort, in this order.
- The late modules (since the first test, 26.10.2026): there the plan was finished at ~485 s and the AI sat at its
  storage caps (1200 metal / 9000 energy) for six minutes while the player's machine guns and mortars wore down the
  lower front. The gallery-roof struts under the two new mortars (x -3500..-3400 and -3300..-3200, y -25) are solid
  bracing in the plan now instead of background bracing. The third woodspam layer hangs further out over the cliff

- The times: in the first test every module came at its time (none was pulled forward: the player's mortars and
  missile came after the artillery and anti-air modules) and the build kept pace (heavy mortars 346 s, miniguns
  570 s). With the late modules there is more to build after ~480 s, but once they are in, the AI only rebuilds.
  In the second test the player's rockets pulled both anti-air modules forward; the first still waited ~90 s for
  the gallery roof (`Requires` 145).

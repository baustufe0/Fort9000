# CS_UNSTABLE probe runs

How the probe works: [debugging.md](../debugging.md). The facts drawn from these runs: [building-rules.md](../building-rules.md).
First game run (24.10.2026, Vanilla-3, 20mm cannon, 60 attempts): removal works (no `CS_OCCUPIED` after it); a device under construction hardly loads the structure (after 3 s `Hold` the strut stress rose 0.026 to 0.029, its nodes dropped 1), so `Hold` tells little; every strut of 30 degrees or more was `CS_INVALIDNODES` (hence `MaxAngle`); on the level struts 2 placed, 13 `CS_UNSTABLE` at strut stress 0.000-0.186 and 12 `CS_NODESTOOCLOSE` (78-164 long, cause unknown); neither strut stress, the stress at its nodes, length nor sag separated placed from refused. Full run the same day (54 level struts, 20mm cannon and sniper at LinkT 0.2/0.5/0.8, 324 attempts): 110 placed, 118 `CS_UNSTABLE`, 96 `CS_NODESTOOCLOSE`; the sniper (mass 40) and the 20mm cannon (100) got the same result on 51 of 54 struts, so the check is not about the device's weight; no measured value separated the results better than 64-71 %. Placed: nearly every level strut of the back block (between fully solid X-braced cells); refused: the starting fort, the front of the tower and the weapon cells, where devices already stand. The probe therefore also logs the existing devices (`dv`, `dn`, `near`); `probe.py` splits the results by them and marks them on the map. Third run with them (same fort, identical totals, so the check is deterministic): every `CS_NODESTOOCLOSE` (96 of 96) was a strut that already carries a device, i.e. the strut is taken; `CS_UNSTABLE` came in 80 of 84 attempts on a strut whose node is shared with a strut carrying a device (placed only 4), against 38 of 144 on struts with no device at either node. Those remaining refusals were 8 struts: four in the sagging, tilted top of the weapon tower (8-13 degrees, sag 56-91), two at its top left (sag 164) and two unexplained (2885,417 and 3499,508); without nearby devices the best single split was sag > 40 (85 %). This holds for adding a device to the finished, loaded fort, but it is not a rule for plans: the game's own plans (Template-1/2, Abyss-1) build turbines and mortars side by side on struts sharing a node, and in the probed match two turbines stood on struts sharing one node (both built while the fort was still small). The refusal near existing devices therefore probably depends on the load at the shared node when the device is added (a device built late, next to loaded devices, fails); not yet a `rules.py` check.

# Vanilla

Current state of the Vanilla plans (`fortplans/Vanilla/`, converted to VanillaLong). The test matches behind it: [history/vanilla-2.md](../history/vanilla-2.md), [history/vanilla-3-5.md](../history/vanilla-3-5.md).

## Starting fort

**Vanilla starting fort** (plan coordinates of the game's `ai/forts/vanilla/mid` plans, team 1, enemy to the
right; weapon_pack's ToGAF1/ToGWM2 use the same fort, shifted by -3950/-3300)
- The rock under the fort reaches from x -3600 to -2900 (top at y=700). The starting fort's prow
  (x -2945..-2845) already overhangs the cliff; anything heavier built out there tears the fort apart.
- Reactor at about (-3040, 305); roof beam from (-3093, 205) to (-2945, 206) with posts at both ends; front
  posts (-2945, 206), (-2943, 312), (-2892, 411) … down to (-2900, 700).
- Proven slots from the game's own plans: battery/store, workshop, upgrade, factory (Template-1/-2),
  munitions plant and a third workshop slot (ToGWM2); mines at x -3029, -3412, -3539 on y=700.
- The starting fort leans forward: build backwards (behind it) and upwards, not out over the cliff.
- The AI does not place a new node at its plan position but at the **current position of its source node plus
  the planned offset** (`GetAdjustedNodePosition` in `data/ai/ai.lua`; on land without rotation). A lean or
  sag of a node while building is therefore copied into every node built from it, and a strut later built
  between nodes from different paths takes their current distance as its rest length (locked-in
  deformation; the `UNSTABLE` strain values far outside 0.90..1.10 come from this). A node that lands more
  than 20 from an existing one becomes a separate node (`SnapToNode(dest, 20)`). Keep the chains of new
  nodes short and anchored in rigid structure; in the stepped Vanilla-4 (since reverted) almost every node of the tower was
  built through one chain of 14-17 nodes up a 100-wide column at the back (x -3500, built first for the first
  turbine), and the tower top was ~200 forward of its plan position after 4.6 minutes, almost without enemy
  fire. A build-only test with `SHAPE` lines confirmed it: the lean grew only while the tower was being
  built (minutes 1-3: rows above y=205 shifted +17 per storey, every node of a row by the same amount) and then
  stayed constant; nodes at row 205 were placed ~23 forward of the nodes directly below them because they
  were built sideways from the leaning column; the cannon cells' back edge (from the chain, 61-79 forward)
  and front edge (from the starting fort, +5) squeezed the 155-wide cells to 85-100, floor beams locked in
  at 42-55 % of their length, so neither cannon could be placed.
- The starting fort already has 2 mines, a battery, a store and a machine gun; their spots are occupied. The
  machine gun stands on the strut (-2996,504)-(-2895,505) of the starting fort's lower front, beside the workshop
  slot (`orig_devices`; a failed sale listed the own devices at match start: `machinegun@64-65:2949,503` for the
  mirrored team 2). Until 25.10.2026 it was assumed on the prow's roof strut (-2945,206)-(-2846,204), from a probe
  log whose machine gun there had been built by the Vanilla-3 plan; that wrong assumption moved devices off the
  prow storey and led to a needless sale in Vanilla-2 (both reverted).

## Shared shape and build order

The Vanilla variants share one shape: a back block on the ground (x -3600..-3100, back to the rock edge) up to the starting fort's roof height (y=205), and a solid, fully X-braced weapon tower (x -3400..-2945, five storeys up to y=-295) on the back block and the starting fort's roof posts; weapon column at the front, the prow beyond the cliff edge carries two light storeys.

The tower roof carries the turbines (one per cell, nothing resting on them) and, in Vanilla-2, the mortars: it is the only height where the wind reaches a turbine from both sides (on the back block's roof the tower blocked the wind on the right of every turbine). (An open turbine storey under the weapons was the breaking point in a test match: one hit dropped the whole upper block.)

They open as in design rule 4 of [building-rules.md](../building-rules.md) (Vanilla-2: the sniper and mortar rush; Vanilla-3: the 20mm rush; Vanilla-5: the laser rush; Vanilla-4: mine, workshop, roof turbine, machine gun, mine), end with a late-game block (more anti-air, weapons, upgrades, storage) so the AI does not sit idle at the storage cap, and assume `weapon_pack` is installed (all players use it): cannons and 20mm cannons need the munitions plant (`prereq` override, munitions slot taken from weapon_pack's own plan ToGWM2). Each variant has its own main weapon, so the player meets a different challenge: Vanilla-2 mortars, Vanilla-3 two 20mm cannons, Vanilla-4 two cannons (both with the munitions plant), Vanilla-5 a laser rush (factory; a battery and a store for the tech's energy and metal costs, the turbines after the laser, upgraded from an upgrade centre on the munitions slot, since the factory takes the usual upgrade slot).

Build order of the tower (all Vanilla variants, `tower_connect` in the map config): the tower stands on two foundations, the back block and the starting fort. Built column by column, its front part went up to the roof alone (for the first turbine), the back part followed and the seam column between them came last, so the two parts leaned apart. Now the cells above y=205 are built storey by storey: a cell in storey k (1 = lowest) waits for every tower cell in storeys 1..max(2, k-2) (`connect_tower`, added to the cell dependencies), so both parts are joined over two storeys before anything higher is built and never rise more than two storeys above the joined part. Cost: the back block comes first, so the first turbine and the first weapon follow about 155-170 struts instead of about 31.

To start faster, every Vanilla variant now opens with the mine under the starting fort (x -3029), the workshop (on the starting fort, no tower needed), the first roof turbine, the anti-air machine gun and then the second mine (x -3412; the third, x -3539, stays later).

## Vanilla-2 – sniper and mortar rush

Vanilla-2 differs (sniper and mortar rush, 24.10.2026): no tall tower; two storeys over the starting fort's roof and the back block (x -3500..-2845; the first one solid, the second one hidden behind background bracing), with a two-storey deck on the starting fort's roof (the front column x -3100..-2945 on its roof posts, and the prow). Its second storey (y 105..-25, 130 high: the variant's rows are 205, 105, -25; 135 would make the 155-wide nest cell's diagonal longer than `MAX_LINK`) is a hidden mortar nest: its floor over the front column is built in two halves through a node at x -3022 (`split_edges`, braced down to the starting fort's roof posts with background bracing), one mortar on each half (`mortar_slots`, at their outer ends, LinkT 0.25 and 0.75: 116 apart); the prow's nest cell is split at half height (`split_cell`) and carries two stacked sandbags (`sandbag_slots`, at y 105 and 40, LinkT 0.7) in front of both mortars against sniper fire; everything else in the nest above its floor (posts, diagonals, roof) background bracing (`hidden_box`), through which the enemy cannot see and the mortar shells pass (the built-in AI checks only the terrain for lobbed weapons, `TargetObstructed`, so the nest does not stop it firing). The nest's sandbags (0 metal / 300 energy) come before the mortars, while the workshop builds (placed after them, both mortars were destroyed 3-5 s later by the player's machine-gun fire through the nest). The sniper stands in the front column on the starting fort's roof beam (-3093,205)-(-2945,206), like Template-2's sniper, and fires through the prow storey (it holds the starting fort's machine gun, door in front; the post between them is recessed by the game when the sniper is placed); `weapon_cells` (`cfg`: `(left x, floor y, device)`) gives it that cell, which is not at the front. The mortars in the tower's top cells had waited for the whole storey-by-storey tower (sniper after 165 struts, mortars after 175-183); now the sniper comes after 14 struts, both mortars after 19 and the sandbags after 33. Behind the nest, on the same hidden storey (`hidden_box` x -3500..-2845), the turbine gallery: three turbines on the floor (`turbine_floor_y` 105, x -3500..-3200), its front cell (x -3200..-3100, next to the nest) split for two more stacked sandbags directly in front of the turbines (`split_cell` takes a list, `sandbag_slots` entries `(split cell index, slot, LinkT)`). Order: the workshop at once (no armour sale: the inner armour in front of the reactor stays, `rebrace_after_mine` off; the prow storey the sniper fires through stays a fire lane with a door and background diagonals, `lane_cells`), the sniper, the deck and the nest's sandbags while the workshop builds (60 s), the mortars as soon as it is finished, all three free mine sites (ground, no struts; energy was never short in the 15th test, metal always), the first turbine, the gallery's sandbags, both woodspam regions (the prow band in front of the reactor and the starting fort's lower front), a machine gun on the gallery roof (anti-air, as soon as that roof stands), two more turbines, then the upgrade centre and, while it builds (60 s; every upgrade waits for it, and the AI waits on a planned upgrade without moving on: in a test match the heavy mortars came 52 s after the upgrade centre was placed, with only a battery in between), a battery, a store and the second machine gun on the gallery roof. Late game, in this order: both mortars upgraded to heavy mortars, the sniper upgraded to the AP sniper, then miniguns (both machine guns on the gallery roof, `roof_weapons` with `roof_weapon_cells` x -3200 and -3400 at y -25, both upgraded), then woodspam (two layers, x -2805 and -2765, in front of the starting fort's prow below the deck, y 205..310, in front of the reactor; foreground bracing, `outside` since no grid cell stands behind that band), then the turbine and mine upgrades. Woodspam stays out of the hidden storey: there it stood 460-600 in front of the two front gallery turbines at their hub height and blocked their front-side wind completely. The AI upgrades whatever device stands on the planned strut (`GetDeviceIdOnPlatform`), so a plan can also upgrade a starting-fort device, and it can sell one (`DESTROY_DEVICE`, as the game's Abyss-1 does) to free its strut. No third mortar: the low fort has no free top cell with an open arc. Without the late block the plan had ended at ~390 s and the AI would only have repaired. The roof turbines of the five-storey tower had waited for the whole back block (first turbine after 163 struts, ~9,700 energy in struts: storeys above the second wait for the first two over the full width, `tower_connect`); in the gallery the first turbine comes after 92 struts (~5,500 energy). With the gallery's front cell holding the sandbags, the turbines need the back block below them; a turbine in that cell itself (hanging on the starting fort's back posts, `support_cheapest`) would come after 63 struts, with only the nest's sandbags in front of it. Wind (`rules.wind_rays`): the back side is open (100%), the front side 36-73% clear (the downward rays meet the solid storey below and the deck), so the middle turbine gets a one-sided warning; how much a turbine with one-sided wind yields is not known. Economy model: sniper at ~12 s, mortars at ~63 s and ~70 s, sandbags ~99 s (metal-limited; mortar cells under door roofs would have cost ~700 metal more for doors and armour).

Variant options for this: `roof_weapons = {"mortar"}` with `roof_weapon_low_first` (the lowest roof with an open arc first; `mortar_open_arc` applies to roof mortars too), `small_low_first` (the sniper in the lowest front cell) and `weapon_pick_best` (no random pick among the best two cells).

  - Vanilla-2 (sniper and mortar rush, hidden nest and gallery; 15 test matches 25.10.2026, details above and in [history/vanilla-2.md](../history/vanilla-2.md)): the
    current layout builds the sniper at ~11 s, the mortars at ~65 s, the first turbine at ~142 s, all four sandbags
    and the upgrades in order. It won against a player twice (390 and 568 s, no device lost) and against an idle
    player (190 s), and lost three times: at 453 s to a laser beam through the unprotected lower front (both mortars had
    been destroyed early, placed before their sandbags), at 338 s to rockets through the reactor's front, and at
    634 s to a laser and a firebeam through the reactor's front (metal-starved, the mines came late). Not yet
    tested: the current version (all three mines right after the mortars); whether the sandbags stop the player's snipers; whether the AI's offence (two mortars, a sniper, miniguns) can
    stop a player's rockets or laser at all (kills so far: machine guns, turbines, one rocket launcher).

## Vanilla-3/-4 – heavy weapons (Vanilla-3: 20mm rush)

Large weapons are reserved first (`reserve_large`) and placed in the two storeys below the top one (`large_top_min`/`large_top_max`: cell tops y=-195 and -95); the top storey gets a sniper and its roof a turbine.

The two-heavy-weapon variants (Vanilla-3, -4) place them like weapon_pack's own ToGWM2 (`large_cells`): the first on the 100-wide cell x -3200..-3100 (floor 5), the second a column back (x -3300..-3200), both at LinkT 0.75, behind the 155-wide front cells; those cells are their fire lanes (`mark_large`: no device, background diagonals, door in the front wall).

The cannon storeys of Vanilla-3/-4 are therefore 125 high (`cfg` overrides `rows`, shape, woodspam rows and `turbine_roof_max_y`); the sniper storey below them (front column, floor 105) too, after a sniper there was refused with `CS_UNSTABLE` in both tests with 100-high storeys: rows 205, 105, -20, -145, -270, -370 (tower roof), the cannons at floors -20 and -145, the 155x125 cells with 199-long diagonals (`MAX_LINK` 205; the game allows `StandardMaxLinkLength` 218).

Vanilla-3/-4 therefore have no prow storeys and no woodspam base on the prow, and the cannons stand at LinkT 0.5.

Still refused with `CS_UNSTABLE`: a sniper in the front column early in the build, and the turbine on the roof cell x -3300..-3200. The sniper (front cell x -3100..-2945 on the starting fort's front post, floor 105) was refused in all three test matches of 24.10.2026 too, and the AI gave up on it after 120 s; `weapon_avoid` (`cfg`: cells `(left x, floor y, devices)` that these weapons skip) now keeps snipers out of that cell in Vanilla-3/-4 (and VanillaLong-3/-4), so the sniper swaps places with the machine gun on the tower's top front cell (floor -270), and the machine gun takes the low cell late in the build.

`turbine_avoid` (`cfg`: roofs `(left x, roof y)` that turbines skip) now leaves that roof without a turbine: Vanilla-3/-4 (and VanillaLong-3/-4) have three roof turbines, which the test match showed is enough for the munitions plant and both heavy weapons.

Vanilla-3 is now a 20mm rush. Economy (game files and the `SNAP` lines): start 601 metal / 3004 energy, reactor and the two starting mines +13 metal/s and about +100 energy/s, storage 600 + 300 per store metal and 3000 + 2000 per battery energy (900 / 5000 at the start); workshop 400 m / 2000 e / 60 s, munitions plant 800 / 5000 / 90 s (needs the workshop), 20mm 600 / 4000 / 70 s (needs the munitions plant), a burst 40 m / 2000 e; wood bracing 0.1 m and 0.5 e per unit length, armour 1.8 m / 1.5 e with 0.9 m back when sold. The tech chain (60 + 90 + 70 s) sets the pace and the starting economy pays for it on time if nothing else comes first (an economy model of the plan order: first shot ~220 s instead of 445-480 s in the test matches; a mine at the start costs ~20 s, since its 2000 energy delays the workshop). Order: the inner armour layer rebuilt as wood at once (plan step `rebrace`), the workshop, the munitions plant (28 struts: its proven slot x -3400..-3300 at y 602; on the bottom floor of the column right behind the starting fort, next to the workshop, it was refused with `CS_UNSTABLE` in the first rush test without any stress or sag - its body beside the workshop, its 170-high box over the workshop's strut - and the AI gave up on it, so the 20mm failed with `CS_PREQUISITENOTMET`), a mine and a battery while it builds, the first 20mm (60 struts in all, model ~226 s; test match 24.10.2026: workshop 65 s, munitions plant placed at once and built at 155 s, the 20mm placed at 158 s and built at 228 s, as modelled, but its first shot came only at ~343 s - from 235 s on the fire control found it not available or not ready although energy and the line of fire were free, cause not yet logged: on floor 105 of the same column, a 125-high storey; its cell is closed above and nothing stands in front of it yet), then the economy, the tower and the second 20mm on floor -20 above it. That column hangs on the starting fort's back posts (about 3 struts per cell); `support_cheapest` (Vanilla-3 `cfg`) makes the generator rest each node on the support with the fewest cells below it instead of the shallowest one, which had led from column to column through the whole back block (100 struts before the cannon instead of 42). Right after the first mine rebuilds the inner metal layer in front of the reactor as wood bracing (`rebrace_after_mine`: starting-fort nodes 50-53 and 53-55, the diagonal (-2892,411)-(-2943,312) and the post (-2943,312)-(-2945,206); a `CREATE_LINK` with another material makes the game swap it, the refunded metal goes into the tower; the outer armour shell at x -2845 stays). A variant can override map settings with `cfg`. A large weapon's LinkT keeps its body inside its cell (`large_t` 0.2 in the 155-wide cell, `large_t_narrow` 0.32 in a 100-wide cell), so the cell behind it keeps solid diagonals and a solid post (`large_rear_solid`).

## Vanilla-3/-4/-5 – turbine cage; Vanilla-5 – laser rush

Vanilla-3/-4/-5 (25.10.2026): the roof turbines stand in a hidden cage, a 130-high storey of background bracing on the tower roof (rows ending at -500), whose front cell (x -3100..-2945) holds two stacked sandbags in front of the turbines, placed right after the first turbine; that cell held a turbine, so Vanilla-3/-4/-5 have two turbines (the seam column refuses them, `turbine_avoid` now also for floor turbines), upgraded late (they build an upgrade centre for it, with storage and anti-air in between). The first turbine comes as early as before (after 152-162 struts, the cage is left out of `tower_connect`); the sandbags' cell costs ~23 struts.

Vanilla-5 (laser rush, 25.10.2026) takes the 20mm rush's layout (`cfg` of Vanilla-3: the tall tower with the cage, `support_cheapest`) with one laser in the rush 20mm's cell (x -3200..-3100, floor 105, 125 high, LinkT 0.5; `large_cells`), firing through the front cell over the reactor; own beams pass the own background bracing (`CollidesWithFriendlyBeams = false`). Economy (game files): factory 800 m / 6000 e / 60 s, laser 1000 m / 6000 e / 120 s, one beam 5000 e (reload 24 s); both cost more energy than the starting cap (5000) and the laser more metal than the metal cap (900). Order: workshop, battery (cap 7000), factory (its proven slot below the laser's column), store (cap 1200) and a mine while it builds, the laser (after 57 struts, as the rush 20mm), then the defence learned from Vanilla-2: woodspam in front of the reactor (prow band y 205..310) and the starting fort's lower front (y 411..700) right after the laser (`woodspam` regions 0 and 1; region 2, the tower front, late), the inner armour in front of the reactor kept (`rebrace_after_mine` off), the anti-air machine gun, the two cage turbines with their sandbags, a mine, the sniper, the upgrade centre on the munitions slot with batteries, a store and the second machine gun while it builds, upgraded turbines, then miniguns, the AP sniper, mines. Not yet tested.

`VANILLA_VARIANTS`; converted to [VanillaLong](vanillalong.md)). Every fact here has its test match in
[history/vanilla-2.md](../history/vanilla-2.md) or [history/vanilla-3-5.md](../history/vanilla-3-5.md); general
rules are in [building-rules.md](../building-rules.md).

## Starting fort

Plan coordinates of the game's `ai/forts/vanilla/mid` plans, team 1, enemy to the right; weapon_pack's ToGAF1/ToGWM2
use the same fort, shifted by -3950/-3300.

- Rock under the fort from x -3600 to -2900 (top y=700). The prow (x -2945..-2845) already overhangs the cliff:
  anything heavy out there tears the fort apart. The fort leans forward, so build backwards and upwards.
- Reactor at about (-3040, 305); roof beam (-3093, 205)-(-2945, 206) with posts at both ends; front posts
  (-2945, 206), (-2943, 312), (-2892, 411) … down to (-2900, 700).
- Starting devices: 2 mines (x -3157, -3284), a battery, a store, a machine gun on the lower front strut
  (-2996,504)-(-2895,505) (`orig_devices`; not on the prow).
- Free mine sites: x -3029, -3412, -3539 on y=700 (a player also mines x -2902, under the front; unused so far).
- Proven slots from the game's plans: battery/store, workshop, upgrade, factory (Template-1/-2), munitions plant and a
  third workshop slot (ToGWM2).
- Economy: start 601 metal / 3004 energy, +13 metal/s (reactor 5, mines 4 each), ~+100 energy/s; caps 900 / 5000.
- `rebrace_after_mine` (map default, nodes 50-53, 53-55): the inner armour layer in front of the reactor is rebuilt as
  wood after the first mine for metal; the outer shell at x -2845 stays. Vanilla-2 and -5 turn it off: in two lost
  Vanilla-2 matches the breach came through exactly that layer. Vanilla-3 does it at once (plan step `rebrace`).
- The breach in every lost Vanilla-2 match came through the reactor's front (laser, rockets, laser + firebeam).

## Shared tower (Vanilla-3/-4/-5)

- **Shape:** a back block on the ground (x -3600..-3100, back to the rock edge) up to the starting fort's roof (y=205),
  and a solid, fully X-braced weapon tower (x -3400..-2945) on the back block and the starting fort's roof posts. No
  prow storeys and no woodspam base on the prow (the prow's armour reached 0.97 of its breaking limit under a cannon).
- **Rows** 205, 105, -20, -145, -270, -370 (tower roof), cage -500: the heavy-weapon storeys and the sniper storey
  below them are 125 high (a heavy weapon's box reaches the floor 100 above once nodes sag, and the game turns the
  overlapped floor into background bracing); 155x125 cells have 199-long diagonals.
- **Storey by storey** (`tower_connect`): a cell in storey k waits for every tower cell in storeys 1..max(2, k-2), so the
  back part (on the back block) and the front part (on the starting fort) are joined before the tower rises; built
  column by column they leaned apart and squeezed the seam column x -3300..-3200. Cost: ~155-170 struts before the
  first roof turbine.
- **Turbine cage** on the tower roof: a 130-high storey of background bracing (`hidden_box` x -3400..-2945, y
  -500..-370), two turbines on its floor and two stacked sandbags in its split front cell (x -3100..-2945) right after
  the first turbine; left out of `tower_connect` (`skip_floors`). Two turbines only (the seam column refuses one,
  `turbine_avoid`), upgraded late from an upgrade centre with storage and anti-air in between. The first turbine
  comes after 152-162 struts; the sandbag cell costs ~23 struts.
- **Heavy weapons** stand at x -3200..-3100 (behind the 155-wide front cells, which are their fire lanes:
  `mark_large`, door in front, background diagonals), LinkT 0.5, one above the other. Stacked in the weapon column with
  100-high storeys, or one column back (the seam), the upper one was refused.
- **Snipers:** never in the front cell on the starting fort's front post (x -3100..-2945, floor 105; refused in every
  match, `weapon_avoid`). Vanilla-3 and -5 put the sniper in the front column at floor -145 (`weapon_avoid` keeps the
  machine gun out; built), at LinkT 0.28 (`sniper_t`) so its split door's brace clears it. Vanilla-4's goes to the
  top front cell (floor -270), where it is refused once the tower stands below it.
- **Doors:** `split_doors` cuts the door posts to the shot: Vanilla-3's 20mm doors 78 instead of 125 (126 shots
  through them), its sniper's 62.5. Cannons (Vanilla-4) and the laser (Vanilla-5) keep full doors (no split covers
  both barrel estimates); in the 100-high storeys no brace clears the sniper, so its door stays full.
- **Woodspam** (region 2): two layers (x -2905/-2865) in front of the tower front, rows -370..-145, late.
- **Late block:** every plan ends with more anti-air, weapons, upgrades and storage, so the AI does not sit idle at the
  storage cap.

## Vanilla-2 – sniper and mortar rush

**Layout.** No tall tower: two storeys over the starting fort's roof and the back block (x -3500..-2845, rows 205,
105, -25), the first solid, the second hidden (`hidden_box` x -3500..-2845, y -25..105, 130 high: a 155-wide cell may
be at most 130 high for `MAX_LINK`).
- **Sniper** in the front column on the starting fort's roof beam (-3093,205)-(-2945,206) at LinkT 0.7 (`weapon_cells`),
  firing through the prow storey (`lane_cells`), whose front post is split (`split_edges` with `materials`): a door
  61.5 long (y 204..142.5, ~1.2 s to open) under 37.5 of armour, muzzle ~44 behind it (124 shots, no clearance failures).
  On the prow itself the sniper was refused.
- **Mortar nest** over the front column: its floor built in two halves through a node at x -3022 (`split_edges`,
  braced down to the roof posts), one mortar per half (`mortar_slots`, LinkT 0.25 and 0.75: 116 apart; 77 apart the
  second was refused). The prow's nest cell is split (`split_cell`) for two stacked sandbags (y 105 and 40, LinkT 0.7)
  in front of both mortars. Shells pass the background bracing and the built-in AI checks only terrain for lobbed
  weapons, so the hidden mortars fire normally (101 shots in a match).
- **Turbine gallery** behind the nest on the same storey: three turbines on its floor (`turbine_floor_y` 105, LinkT
  0.35: x -3465, -3365, -3265), its front cell (x -3200..-3100) split for two stacked sandbags (lower ones at x -3170,
  90 from the first mortar). Wind: back side open, front side 36-73 % clear (one-sided warning for the middle
  turbine). The first turbine comes after ~92 struts instead of ~163 on a tower roof.
- **Machine guns** on the gallery roof (`roof_weapons`, `roof_weapon_cells` x -3200 and -3400 at y -25), not on the
  nest roof (in the shells' path); both become miniguns. The starting machine gun stays (not in the sniper's way).
- **Woodspam:** region 0 two layers (x -2805/-2765) in front of the prow below the deck (y 205..310, in front of the
  reactor, foreground bracing, `outside`); region 1 two layers (x -2852/-2812) hanging in front of the starting fort's
  lower face (y 411..700, background bracing in the band 411..505 where the starting machine gun fires). Never at the
  gallery's height (it blocked the turbines' wind completely).
- No third mortar (no free top cell with an open arc); inner armour in front of the reactor kept (`rebrace_after_mine`
  off).

**Build order** (why in brackets):
1. Workshop at once (it sets the pace: 60 s); while it builds the sniper (100 m, no tech), the deck and the nest's
   sandbags (0 m / 300 e) - the sandbags must stand before the mortars (machine-gun fire passes the background bracing).
2. Both mortars as soon as the workshop is finished (~63-66 s).
3. All three free mine sites (metal, not energy, was the limit: with the mines after the upgrade centre metal stayed
   below 150 most of the match and the plan stalled for 4 minutes while energy sat at its cap).
4. First gallery turbine, the gallery's sandbags, both woodspam regions, the machine gun on the gallery roof, two more
   turbines.
5. Upgrade centre, and while it builds (60 s; every upgrade waits for it) a battery, a store, the second machine gun.
6. Late: heavy mortars, AP sniper, miniguns, woodspam again, upgraded turbines, a battery, upgraded mines, woodspam.

Without the late block the plan ended at ~390 s. Economy model: sniper ~12 s, mortars ~74/89 s with the armour kept.

**Results so far:** won twice (390 s and 568 s, no device lost) and against an idle player (190 s); lost at 453 s
(laser through the then unprotected lower front, mortars killed before their sandbags), 338 s (rockets through the
reactor's front) and 634 s (laser + firebeam through the reactor's front; metal-starved, the AI never hit the
beams while they were being built). Not yet known: whether the sandbags stop the player's snipers, whether two mortars,
a sniper and miniguns can stop a player's rockets or laser at all (kills so far: machine guns, turbines, one rocket
launcher).

## Vanilla-3 – 20mm rush

- **Order:** the inner armour rebuilt as wood at once (`rebrace`), workshop, munitions plant (its proven slot
  x -3400..-3300 at y 602, 28 struts; beside the workshop it was refused), a mine and a battery while it builds, the
  first 20mm (floor 105, x -3200..-3100, after ~58-60 struts), then the cage turbine and its sandbags, anti-air, a mine,
  the sniper, the second turbine, a mine, the upgrade centre (store, battery, machine gun, sniper while it builds),
  upgraded turbines, woodspam, the second 20mm (floor -20), anti-air, woodspam.
- **Economy:** the tech chain workshop 60 s + munitions plant 90 s (800 m / 5000 e: the whole energy cap) + 20mm 70 s
  sets the pace; the starting economy pays for it if nothing else comes first (model: first shot ~225 s). A mine at
  the start costs ~20 s (its 2000 energy delays the workshop).
- **`support_cheapest`:** the 20mm's column hangs on the starting fort's back posts (~3 struts per cell) instead of
  pulling in the whole back block (100 struts before the cannon with the default support choice, 42 with this).
- Measured: munitions plant placed 65 s, 20mm placed 155-158 s, built ~225 s, fires at once once the post in front of
  it is a door (`SWAP_WINDOW`); cage turbines 268 and 359 s, upgrade centre 414 s, second 20mm 478 s; energy plenty
  (~10,500 at the end), metal short until late.

## Vanilla-5 – laser rush

- Vanilla-3's layout with one laser in the rush 20mm's cell (x -3200..-3100, floor 105, LinkT 0.5), firing through the
  front cell over the reactor; own beams pass own background bracing (`CollidesWithFriendlyBeams = false`). The factory
  takes its proven slot below that column. The upgrade centre stands one strut behind the munitions slot
  (x -3500..-3400, y 602, LinkT 0.5): on the munitions slot it overlapped the factory and was refused; the batteries
  and stores keep clear of it (y 503).
- The laser's fire lane (the cell ahead of it, x -3100..-2945, with its split door) is built right before the laser:
  built later, the laser stood in an open cell and was sniped before it was finished.
- The laser cell's front post (x -3100, floor 105 to -20) is background bracing: the laser's body reaches only 50 ahead
  and its recession box starts 130 ahead, so the solid post 50 in front of it blocked every shot in the first test.
- Costs: factory 800 m / 6000 e / 60 s, laser 1000 m / 6000 e / 120 s, one beam 5000 e (reload 24 s): more energy than
  the starting cap and more metal than the metal cap.
- Order: workshop, battery (cap 7000), factory, then while it builds a store (cap 1200) and all three free mine sites,
  the anti-air machine gun on the starting fort's prow roof strut (`weapon_cells`, one light cell, no tower struts),
  the laser's lane and the laser (action 78); while it builds woodspam regions 0 and 1 (as Vanilla-2, in front of the
  reactor and the lower front), the cage turbines with their sandbags, a battery, the sniper (front column, floor
  -145); then the upgrade centre (battery, store, second machine gun while it builds), upgraded turbines, miniguns,
  the AP sniper, the tower's woodspam, upgraded mines. Inner armour kept. Before the rework (25.10.2026) only one mine
  and no anti-air came before the laser: the AI was broke and a player's rockets destroyed the laser 3 s after it was
  finished.
- First test (one match, difficulty 1): the build ran as planned up to the upgrade centre (workshop 3 s, factory 80 s,
  laser placed 219 s, built 339 s, cage turbines 369/427 s, sniper 459 s). The upgrade centre on the munitions slot
  was refused (`CS_OCCUPIED`, then `CS_UNSTABLE`), so the upgraded turbines never came and the AI sat at its storage
  caps (1500 / 11,000) from ~545 s. The player's laser and firebeam, fired together once at ~626 s, cut the lower
  front with its woodspam and took the reactor in under 4 s; the AI destroyed nothing.
- Module `counterbattery` (enemy has 2 mortars or heavy mortars): two mortars on the hidden cage's roof struts
  (x -3400..-3300 and -3200..-3100, y -500), which the plan now builds as solid bracing instead of background bracing.
  The tower blocks the mortar arc of every place behind or below it; the prow roof is open but in the laser's line of
  fire. So the module can only come once the plan has built the cage roof (`Requires` 235 of 307); at the last
  match's pace that is about 10 minutes in. The player's mortars (25.10.2026) hit the prow cell with the anti-air
  machine gun and the front woodspam, not the back block, so an armour roof over the back block would not have helped.

## Vanilla-6 – modular fort

- The idea: a fixed plan loses to whatever the player picks against it (Vanilla-5 lost to four different weapons).
  Vanilla-6 keeps a fixed core and turns its weapons into reactive build modules ([ai.md](../ai.md#reactive-build-modules)).
- Geometry: Vanilla-2's low fort, strut for strut (the most tested layout; the hidden mortar nest and turbine gallery
  two storeys over the starting fort, nothing in the mortars' arc); the weapons stand where Vanilla-2's stand.
- Core (231 actions): workshop; the sniper's cell (built for a sniper: door, background diagonals) and the nest cell
  with the split mortar struts (`build` step, before anything else builds them); the nest's sandbags; three mines;
  a turbine; the gallery's sandbags; woodspam; two turbines; upgrade centre; battery, store; upgraded turbines; a
  battery; upgraded mines; woodspam.
- Modules (enemy devices, or at the latest the time; `Requires` = plan action):

  | Module | Builds | Enemy builds | Time | Requires |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | sniper | sniper on the starting fort's roof beam | sniper, AP sniper, laser, firebeam, cannon, 20mm | 45 s | 5 |
  | artillery | two mortars in the nest | mortar, heavy mortar | 150 s | 30 |
  | antiair | machine gun on the gallery roof | missile, EMP missile, rocket, EMP rocket | 240 s | 145 |
  | heavyartillery | heavy mortars (after artillery) | - | 330 s | 199 |
  | antiair2 | second gallery-roof machine gun (after antiair) | 3 of the missile types | 420 s | 192 |
  | sniper2 | AP sniper (after sniper) | 2 AP snipers, lasers or firebeams | 450 s | 199 |
  | miniguns | both machine guns upgraded (after antiair2) | - | 540 s | 199 |
  | artillery2 | two more mortars on the gallery roof, between the machine guns (after heavyartillery) | - | 480 s | 189 |
  | heavyartillery2 | their upgrade (after artillery2) | - | 560 s | 199 |
  | frontlayer | a third woodspam layer (x -2772) in front of the starting fort's lower front (y 411..700) | 6 machine guns or miniguns | 600 s | 167 |

  Against a player who builds nothing the AI still reaches its goal, Vanilla-2's artillery fort, in this order.
- The late modules (since the first test, 26.10.2026): there the plan was finished at ~485 s and the AI sat at its
  storage caps (1200 metal / 9000 energy) for six minutes while the player's machine guns and mortars wore down the
  lower front. The gallery-roof struts under the two new mortars (x -3500..-3400 and -3300..-3200, y -25) are solid
  bracing in the plan now instead of background bracing. The third woodspam layer hangs further out over the cliff
  (more load on the starting fort's lower front); whether the front holds it is untested.
- Anti-air on the gallery roof needs the gallery's front cell (~90 actions of support from the back block): it cannot
  come before plan action 145; until then only the starting fort's machine gun covers the sky.
- The times: in the first test every module came at its time (none was pulled forward: the player's mortars and
  missile came after the artillery and anti-air modules) and the build kept pace (heavy mortars 346 s, miniguns
  570 s). With the late modules there is more to build after ~480 s, but once they are in, the AI only rebuilds.

## Open problems

- Vanilla-4's sniper on the top front cell (floor -270) is refused once the tower stands below it.
- Vanilla-3's sniper behind its split door and Vanilla-4's cannons against a player are not yet tested; the cannons' doors stay full until a test shows where their barrel is.

  stops (EMP) rockets, and whether the laser survives rockets from above at all, is open.
- Two beams fired together (laser + firebeam) cut two woodspam layers and the starting fort's lower front in under
  4 s; woodspam does not stop that.
- One armour piece of a 20mm door post (47 long, y 27..-20) was refused with `CS_NODESTOOCLOSE` and given up; the
  other door's identical piece was built. Cause unknown.
- The AI has not yet beaten a human who builds heavy weapons by ~6 minutes at difficulty 1 (Vanilla-2 lost to a laser,
  rockets, and a laser with a firebeam); it never targets a laser or firebeam while it is being built.
- A player mines a sixth site (x -2902, under the front); the AI does not use it (close to woodspam region 1).

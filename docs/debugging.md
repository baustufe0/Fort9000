# FORT 9000 – Debugging

## Debugging

Build functions (`CreateLink`, `CreateNode`, `CreateDeviceWithFlags`, `CreateGroundDevice`, `UpgradeDevice`, `DestroyDevice`) are hooked; a sale (`DestroyDevice`) that finds nothing on the strut is tried with the nodes the other way round (a device is found only in the node order it was built with, `GetDeviceIdOnPlatform`; the built-in `DestroyAttachedDevices` does the same), if both orders find nothing (test matches 25.10.2026: the planned sale of the starting machine gun found nothing in either order - it stood on another strut, see [the Vanilla starting fort](maps/vanilla.md#starting-fort)), the own device whose platform (`GetDevicePlatformA/B`) is that strut is sold by its id (`DestroyDeviceById`), else the own device standing closest to the strut within 40 (`GetDevicePosition`; in the eleventh test no device's platform matched either: `scan=6 match=none`); a failed sale is logged as `BUILD_ERROR destroy scan=<devices> match=<id|none>` with its `DD_*` code and, if nothing matched, every own device as `type@platform:position`; failed attempts are written to the log with the error code as `BUILD_ERROR` (once per case, max. 200).
A device that fails with `CS_UNSTABLE` additionally writes `UNSTABLE <device> <nodeA>-<nodeB> N<id> sag=<distance from planned position> v=<speed> ... links=<n> strain=<min>..<max> stretched=<link> compressed=<link> action=<k>` (up to 3 samples per device and place): strain is current/planned length of all links at the two nodes (bracing breaks below 0.90 or above 1.10, background bracing below 0.92 or above 1.08); `<link>` = `N<id>(x,y)-N<id>(x,y) <planned material>` for the most stretched / most compressed link (current world positions; `start-fort` if the plan does not build it). Background bracing cannot carry devices (`SupportsDevices = false`).
Shape snapshots: `SHAPE t=<s> why=<minute|unstable:<device>> mirrored=<0|1> part=<k>/<n> <plan node id>:<dx>,<dy> ...` lists how far every plan node (starting fort and new) has moved from its planned position, on the first `CS_UNSTABLE` of a device and place and once per game minute (at most `CAI_SHAPE_MAX` = 40 snapshots, 25 nodes per line). dx/dy are in the plan's own frame (x mirrored back when the game mirrored the plan; `MirrorFort` sets `CAI_FortMirrored`), so they can be laid over the plan file: `python tools/fortgen/shape.py [log] [--team 102] [--match -1] [--png out.png]` prints per snapshot the average forward/down movement of each storey row and the nodes that moved most, and draws the offsets as arrows.
Real stress: on the same first `CS_UNSTABLE` of a device and place (at most `CAI_STRESS_MAX` = 20 times) the mod also enumerates the fort's struts with the engine's own stress (`EnumerateLinks`, callback `CAI_EnumStress`) and writes `STRESS <device> <a>-<b> links=<n> at=<link> ... top=<link> ...`: `at` = every strut at the device's two nodes, `top` = the `CAI_STRESS_TOP` = 8 most stressed struts of the fort (by |stress|); `<link>` = `<node>-<node>:<material>:<stress>` with `P<plan node id>` for plan nodes, else `N<actual id>`. The strain in the `UNSTABLE` line only compares with plan lengths, which is meaningless for struts built between nodes that had already moved; `shape.py` prints the `STRESS` lines with plan positions.
CS_UNSTABLE probe (testing only): with `Probe` set in `fortplans.lua` (see [the configuration reference](ai.md#configuration-reference)), the AI stops (`data.Disable`) once its plan is finished (or after `Probe.At` seconds) and tries every device of `Probe.Devices` at every LinkT of `Probe.LinkT` on every strut of its fort made of `Probe.Materials` (default bracing and armour) and at most `MaxAngle` (default 20) degrees steep (optional `Box` in world coordinates, `Max` attempts), one attempt every `Interval` seconds (default 0.2), one device over the whole fort before the next. It calls the engine's `CreateDeviceWithFlags` directly (no `BUILD_ERROR`/`UNSTABLE` diagnostics) and removes a placed device again at once (refunded), or after `Hold` seconds to measure its load. Lines: `PROBE START`, `PROBE NODE` (every node with world position and plan node id), `PROBE LINK` (every strut with the engine's stress before the probe and the devices standing on it, `<device>@<LinkT>`), one `PROBE TRY <k> <device> <a>-<b> t=<LinkT> r=<OK|result> s=<strut stress> sn=<largest |stress| of the other struts at its nodes> len= ang= sag= v= dv=<devices on the strut> dn=<devices on the other struts at its nodes> near=<their types>` per attempt (measured just before it), `PROBE LOAD` (stress and node movement after `Hold`), `PROBE WAIT` (short of resources: the attempt is repeated, at most 30 times), `PROBE END`; exact format in the header of the probe section of `ai/ai.lua`. `python tools/fortgen/probe.py [log] [--csv out.csv] [--png out.png]` prints per device how many attempts were placed or refused, the quartiles of every measured value for placed vs. `CS_UNSTABLE` and the single threshold that separates them best, lists the refused struts, writes every attempt as CSV (for fitting a model of the check) and draws a map per device. A match with `PROBE` lines is not used for training.
